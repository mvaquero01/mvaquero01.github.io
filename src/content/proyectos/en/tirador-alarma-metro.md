## The problem

Start from a physical part, the emergency handle of a metro carriage, with no drawings, and reproduce it accurately.

## How I did it

- Measurements with a calliper.
- 3D model in SolidWorks.
- Detailed drawings of the part.
- 3D print of the replica.
